How Tandem routes a system

Tandem asks one question before anything else: how is this AI system coming into the organization? Built, bought or turned on inside a tool you already use. The answer decides who registers it, which questions they get and who reviews it.

Implementers

The person or team bringing the system in. They register it and move it through the lifecycle.

Builder

An engineer or data scientist who built the system internally. Knows the model and fills in the technical fields directly.

Procurer

An HR manager, ops lead or business unit owner buying a vendor's tool: a hiring platform, a performance system, an assistant. May not know the model's internals, so Tandem asks what the vendor provided instead.

Enabler

An IT admin or department head turning on a feature inside an approved platform, like Gemini in Google Workspace or Copilot in Microsoft 365. The system already exists; what's reviewed is the decision to turn it on here.

A procurer can't tell you the training data source, but can tell you what the vendor's data processing agreement says and whether a third-party audit exists. An enabler can't describe the model, but can say what data the feature touches. The review still happens; it's built around what each person actually holds.

Reviewers

The teams that check whether a system fits the organization's policies before it ships.

Policy reviewer

Checks the system against internal policies and vendor terms. When a procurer buys a hiring tool, the vendor contract and DPA become the main documents.

Privacy & ethics reviewer

Reviews bias, fairness, the privacy impact assessment and effects on the people involved. May ask the vendor for a bias audit as a condition of approval, even if the procurer didn't have one at intake.

Security / technical reviewer

For bought or enabled systems, often IT security or a technical lead who didn't build it but can assess integrations, data flows and access controls.

Owners and coordinators

People who decide, coordinate or watch, without reviewing intake answers field by field.

Accountable owner

Holds final deployment authority. For a hiring tool, that may be the CHRO or VP of Talent, not the HR business partner who bought it.

Governance lead

Keeps the organization's approved policies loaded in Tandem, shows where two policies overlap, and routes findings to the right owner. A coordinating role, not a legal one.

Community liaison

Represents or stays in contact with the people the system affects. For a company-wide Gemini rollout, that may include employee representatives.

Executive / governance committee

Sees totals across the registry, open blocks and override history. Decides disputed blocks. Doesn't take part in individual reviews.

The tree branches on how the system arrives before anything else, because that decides what Tandem can ask and who it needs to involve.

Pick a type to see how the intake changes. Same review, different questions, matched to what each person can actually know.
